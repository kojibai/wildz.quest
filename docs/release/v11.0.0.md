# Wildz v11.0.0 — The Open Wilds

Source release date: 2026-09-27. Baseline: the first public `v10.0.0` tag. Application version: `11.0.0`. Installed PWA coordinate: `v11.0.0-r1`. The [source ledger](v11.0.0-source-ledger.md) records every implementation commit since that baseline. Receiz SDK, MCP server, and AI skills remain exact public `127.0.0`.

## The experience

Players can enter the outer world through the existing map. Exact decimal region addresses and local coordinates keep travel and terrain deterministic at distances beyond a conventional fixed map grid. World generation and the exploration atlas do bounded work around the current region; the address parser limits each region coordinate to 4,096 digits to bound untrusted input cost. The existing central world, its places, and saved locations remain available.

New starters have Trail rarity. New outer-world sites are generated from their exact address. A discovered site waits for authenticated encounter admission; offline observations remain in a bounded local queue and cannot award a rarity or card. An admitted site enters the existing battle and complete ball-capture sequence. The resulting portable card carries its signed birth; the existing living-card history, save, PNG export/import, and first-meeting homecoming paths carry that evidence forward.

The public [World](https://wildz.quest/world), [Creatures](https://wildz.quest/creatures), and [Rarity & Proof](https://wildz.quest/conformance) pages explain the mechanics without internal migration terminology. The Rarity & Proof page can verify a signed encounter and replay the creature birth offline after the proof is available. Undiscovered site outcomes are not revealed by the public pages.

## Exact rarity law

Each eligible site has a chance at every class. The band is determined by the site's region distance from the starting region, not by client-reported lifetime travel. The signed result uses an unbiased draw from 10,000,000 positions; the counts below come from the executable law.

| Site band | Rare | Mythic | Eternal |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Origin, under 25 regions | 1 in 10,000 | 1 in 1,000,000 | 1 in 10,000,000 |
| Wilds, 25–124 regions | 1 in 5,000 | 1 in 500,000 | 1 in 5,000,000 |
| Deep Wilds, 125–499 regions | 1 in 2,000 | 1 in 200,000 | 1 in 2,000,000 |
| Frontier, 500 or more regions | 1 in 1,000 | 1 in 100,000 | 1 in 1,000,000 |

Uncommon is 1 in 5 in every band; Trail takes the remaining draws. These are per admitted site, not a promise that a player will find a class after a particular number of attempts. An Eternal outcome at the frontier has exactly ten winning draws in ten million.

The signed encounter fixes the address, owner, site slot, rarity draw, and creature seed. The public generator derives anatomy, surface, temperament, habitat, voice, abilities, and base stats from that seed. A creature's exact birth and asset identity can be replayed from its proof; later history is appended to that same card. Rarity is scarcity, not a monetary price or a stat advantage.

## Old-card continuity

Previously admitted cards keep their exact canonical proof bytes, catalog form, rarity, owner verification, and append-only history. Their first-meeting place is read from the existing proven discovery identity or canonical hotspot; an unproven place is not invented. The additional continuity envelope does not rewrite the card. The signed new-birth branch enters the same living-card machinery after capture, while older cards stay on their original verifier path.

The local signed playthrough checks an existing card's canonical bytes across a new creature capture, save/restore, and import. It also checks the new card's original portable proof, living battle revision, PNG round trip, selected form, and birthplace. Stripping the signed birth from a new living card fails offline verification.

## Qualification and activation

The source gate is `pnpm release:check`: architecture lock, full regression suite, typecheck, official Receiz v127 repository and MCP checks, lint, secret scan, optimized production build, and doctor. The [qualification record](v11.0.0-qualification.md) carries the exact run results and remaining evidence boundaries.

Live site admission requires the existing authenticated Receiz player session and private player-state source, a player-scoped remote admission capability, and `WILDZ_V11_ENCOUNTER_SIGNING_KEY_PKCS8_B64` on the server. Its matching public key is pinned in source. The signing key must never be exposed to the browser or committed. The local doctor reports `liveApi: needs-env`; this source gate does not claim a production Receiz admission, device-wide frame-time guarantee, Git tag, GitHub release, push, or deployment. Those require separate operational evidence after the user pushes and configures production.

The source-backed address is the authenticated player's stored state. It is not independent physical-location proof; browser-reported movement can be written into that state. The signed outcome is authentic to the server's admitted input, while the true location of a human device is outside this protocol's guarantees.
