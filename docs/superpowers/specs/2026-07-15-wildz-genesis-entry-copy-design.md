# Wildz Genesis Entry Copy Design

## Outcome

The gender-selection screen presents Wildz as powerful but approachable in one mobile-safe line. Visible Kai Pulse terminology is removed from the entry flow without changing the deterministic internal character identity logic.

## Approved presentation

- Subtitle: “Catch, grow, own, and cash out creatures you can take anywhere.”
- The subtitle remains one line at supported mobile widths through responsive typography and no-wrap layout.
- Gender buttons retain their direct “Female explorer” and “Male explorer” labels without Kai Pulse helper text.
- The short reveal status reads “Shaping your explorer.”

## Verification

- Source tests reject visible Kai Pulse copy in the Genesis component.
- Browser verification checks the subtitle at a 390 px mobile viewport and confirms it does not wrap or overflow.
