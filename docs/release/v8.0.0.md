# Wildz v8.0.0 — Proof-Native Living World

Release date: 2026-08-20. Application version: `8.0.0`. Installed PWA coordinate: `v8.0.0-r1`. Receiz integration: exact public `@receiz/sdk@121.0.0`, `@receiz/mcp-server@121.0.0`, and `@receiz/ai-skills@121.0.0`.

## The release in one sentence

Wildz v8 makes the admitted Proof Object the uninterrupted center of identity, complete game restoration, Vault custody, profiles, and immediate play—then gives that continuity a richer, more embodied living world.

This is not a server-account release disguised as artifact portability. Once exact bytes have been verified and admitted, the Proof Object is authority. A server can verify transport input, publish a sanitized projection, coordinate shared effects, or help another device find the object; it does not become the source of identity or card truth. A database, cookie, cache, publication response, proof-brain index, MCP result, AI explanation, and rendered UI remain subordinate projections.

## What players receive

- Saving an Identity Seal preserves the Receiz identity plus the complete Wildz continuity carried by that saved artifact. Uploading it restores that player and game state instead of briefly switching and falling back to the previously mounted account.
- Uploading one creature card imports and selects that exact card. It does not silently import other cards embedded in unrelated player inventory. Uploading a complete Vault remains the explicit full-collection path.
- Profiles become publication-ready from authenticated game state rather than from whether the Profile panel happens to be open. The owner view immediately uses admitted local truth while remote publication proceeds separately.
- The Profile Vault projects the admitted collection, the selected-card viewer stays compact even for large Vaults, and the complete card back renders its QR image again.
- Post-login play avoids repeated artifact inspection, per-card client verification, duplicate publishing, and avoidable render/state work.
- Creatures carry genome-derived anatomy into the live world, while the atlas and world gain deeper terrain, watercourses, organic placement, lighting, fog, and quality-scaled detail.

## The Proof Object authority model

The v8 authority sequence is deliberately short:

```text
bounded bytes
  → inspect and admit the exact artifact once
  → activate its verified identity and Wildz continuity
  → materialize the admitted Vault and selected card locally
  → begin gameplay immediately
  → publish sanitized projections idempotently when connected
```

The last step never points backward. Publication may make admitted truth discoverable, but it cannot authorize the local identity that produced it, replace the exact local card with a weaker projection, or make gameplay wait for a database acknowledgement.

V8 retains exact card-object references inside an opaque same-runtime admission. The reference, not a matching caller-shaped copy, is eligible for the fast path. Profile and background card publishers can therefore reuse admission without reopening IndexedDB, loading an identity keyfile for each card, or invoking client verification again. Objects crossing a process boundary still require exact-byte verification; same-runtime reuse is not a cross-process trust shortcut.

The public card route accepts a proof projection, verifies its public representation, and transports it through the configured Receiz public store. It no longer treats a cookie actor or server-side Identity key as the card's publication authority. A weaker publication response cannot replace the admitted local Proof Object.

Evidence: `src/lib/receiz/wildz-vault-card-admission.ts`, `src/features/play/public-card-registry.ts`, `src/features/play/use-public-card-publisher.ts`, `src/features/play/PlayCampaign.tsx`, and `app/api/cards/[assetId]/route.ts`; regression coverage in `tests/wildz-vault-card-admission.test.ts`, `tests/wildz-public-card-publisher.test.ts`, and `tests/wildz-public-card-session.test.ts`.

## Complete identity and game-state restoration

Identity activation and Vault merge are separate operations with different scopes.

An Identity Seal may activate only the verified identity and continuity it carries. When that artifact is a Wildz-saved continuity seal, v8 restores its exact saved player projection—including its game history, settings, active selection, and admitted collection—under the embedded Receiz identity. The mounted account can no longer overwrite that continuity with stale data during the restore transition.

A matching Identity Seal may authenticate an already-loaded proof Vault without dropping its current admitted cards. A different verified identity becomes active according to explicit restore rules, not because a browser cookie or an old local label outranks the uploaded artifact. Protected identity authority is never serialized into the public profile or gameplay checkpoint.

The upload adapter now creates a `WildzPreparedRestore`: bounded bytes are read once and the artifact is inspected once. That prepared result moves through the shell and surface-specific restore path without a second inspection. This removes duplicate work without changing the fail-closed admission boundary.

Evidence: `src/features/identity/wildz-restore.ts`, `src/features/shell/WildzApp.tsx`, `src/lib/receiz/wildz-identity-adapter.ts`, and `src/lib/receiz/wildz-profile-adapter.ts`; regression coverage in `tests/wildz-artifact-codec.test.ts`, `tests/wildz-full-vault-regression.test.ts`, and `tests/wildz-vault-login-integration.test.ts`.

## Exact card and Vault movement

V8 makes the importer's visible intent match its actual scope:

| Player action | Admitted result |
|---|---|
| Upload one card in the card Vault | Import and select that exact admitted card; do not import inventory hidden in carried player continuity |
| Upload a card-only Vault | Admit its verified cards under collection merge and conflict laws without changing identity authority |
| Upload an identity-bearing complete Vault | Restore its verified identity/continuity and complete admitted collection according to the identity activation path |
| Export one card | Save the exact selected card artifact |
| Export the Vault or Identity Seal | Preserve the complete eligible collection and player continuity for that export surface |

Additive collection behavior remains exact, not permissive. Exact duplicates are dropped, causal verified descendants may advance, structural conflicts fail closed, and a divergent self-asserted proof cannot win by timestamp or local suffix. The historical 97/98-card regression boundary remains covered.

Cross-platform asset construction now tracks and prefers primary PNG asset IDs, preventing a broad carried asset list from redefining the explicit card being saved or imported.

Evidence: `src/lib/receiz/wildz-cross-platform-cards.ts`, `src/features/identity/wildz-restore.ts`, and `src/features/shell/WildzApp.tsx`; regression coverage in `tests/wildz-full-vault-regression.test.ts`, `tests/wildz-artifact-codec.test.ts`, and `tests/wildz-global-ownership.test.ts`.

## Profiles that reflect admitted truth

Profile publication readiness is now one shared rule: identity, character continuity, and proof-session connectivity must exist. `WildzApp` owns a dedicated readiness-driven publication effect, so opening or closing the Profile panel is not the trigger. Duplicate post-login publication paths were removed.

The owner profile is painted immediately from admitted local identity and Vault truth. Card publication happens before a non-empty public profile projection and yields between bounded background work. Publication status still describes the remote projection honestly: a local verified profile can exist while its public projection is unavailable, rejected, or still pending. That state does not demote local authentication.

When card truth changes, the durable Vault save remains pending while the continuity/profile projection refreshes without forcing movement state through the shell. The owner gallery admits only exact assets present in its local public Vault index. Remote profiles continue to require exact verified published records and cannot receive private Vault entries or identity authority.

The selected profile-card scene is fixed and centered with constrained dimensions, so a large gallery uses its native vertical scroller instead of stretching the viewer to the collection's full height. Profile card backs now receive an actual generated QR PNG rather than a standalone-page URL where image data was required.

Evidence: `src/features/profile/public-profile.ts`, `src/lib/receiz/wildz-profile-adapter.ts`, `src/features/profile/WildzProfileVaultGallery.tsx`, `src/features/profile/profile-vault-card.ts`, `src/features/shell/WildzApp.tsx`, and `app/globals.css`; regression coverage in `tests/wildz-profile.test.ts`, `tests/wildz-profile-vault-gallery.test.ts`, `tests/wildz-public-profile-adapter.test.ts`, and `tests/wildz-save-scheduler.test.ts`.

## Immediate gameplay after admission

V8 removes duplicate work from the upload, identity, publication, state, and render paths while keeping verification at the boundary where it belongs.

- One file read and inspection produces a prepared restore reused by every restore surface.
- One Vault admission is passed from the shell into `PlayCampaign`; the campaign does not derive the same admission again.
- Background card publication reuses admitted object authority rather than verifying every card again.
- Concurrent publishers share one registration for the same card revision, and post-login profile publication has one readiness-driven owner.
- Nearby creature region lookups are cached while movement remains in the same region.
- The frame-time window uses a circular buffer instead of repeated array slicing.
- Production builds do not start the diagnostics publisher.
- D-pad knob movement writes directly through a DOM ref rather than scheduling React state for every pointer change.
- Consecutive inputs do not recreate the save timer, and card-truth saves remain distinct from lightweight movement checkpoints.
- The world canvas suspends when covered; distant remote players and trainers are culled; routes are merged; repeated arena and terrain forms use instancing and LOD-aware counts.
- Inventory selection updates synchronize paint while expensive admission projection is deferred and cached outside movement.

These changes do not declare verification unnecessary. They establish that exact admission is performed once at the correct boundary and then reused locally. Bytes arriving from another process, device, or transport still require verification.

Evidence: `src/features/play/game-state.ts`, `src/features/play/WildzDpad.tsx`, `src/features/play/WildsWorldCanvas.tsx`, `src/features/play/WildsInventory.tsx`, `src/features/play/wilds-quality-governor.ts`, and `src/lib/receiz/wildz-save-scheduler.ts`; regression coverage in `tests/wildz-mobile-performance.test.ts`, `tests/wilds-quality-governor.test.ts`, `tests/wilds-render-contract.test.ts`, and `tests/wildz-card-rail-ui.test.ts`.

## A richer living world

The v8 visual work is procedural and identity-driven rather than decorative replacement.

Atlas and world terrain now share geographic topography and terrain-height projection. Routes follow land shape; river ribbons and watercourses occupy the world; organic island geometry, golden-angle placement, and golden-ratio spacing reduce mechanical repetition. ACES tone mapping, revised camera framing, layered light, fog, emissive readability, and tuned materials produce a more coherent landscape across day, night, and authored weather.

Ecology and terrain-detail counts honor the quality profile. Distant sites, trainers, and remote players use tighter visibility bounds; repeated geometry uses merged buffers or instances; suspended scenes stop spending frames. The result is higher visual density where it is readable and bounded work where it is not.

`CardKaiAppearance` now carries anatomy derived from the exact creature genome. Live creature actors and Hearttree receive explicit body, appendage, and surface traits, so silhouettes and motion remain tied to the individual card rather than collapsing into a family-only model.

Evidence: `src/features/play/WildsAtlasCanvas.tsx`, `src/features/play/WildsEnvironment.tsx`, `src/features/play/WildsCreatureActor.tsx`, `src/features/play/card-kai-appearance.ts`, and `src/features/play/hearttree/HearttreeScene.tsx`; regression coverage in `tests/wildz-v3-appearance.test.ts`, `tests/wilds-creature-thumbnails.test.ts`, and `tests/wilds-render-contract.test.ts`.

## Preserved laws and compatibility

- The enclosing admitted Proof Object remains strongest truth.
- First admission only, then append forever remains the durable-memory law.
- Unknown proof namespaces remain byte-exact through projection, restore, and transfer.
- Historical artifacts remain eligible for v121 verification and documented verified-legacy reads; historical runtime admissions or sessions do not authorize new work.
- The `wildz.creature-care.v7.0.0` ruleset coordinate remains unchanged because it names deterministic historical law, not the current application package.
- Exact-head creature memory, deterministic care and mortality, living-subject Twin behavior, local/offline acoustic rendering, card/Vault custody, roaming, capture, competition, market settlement, and Kai temporal authority are preserved.
- Server/database projections accelerate discovery, distribution, and restore only. Publication failure cannot invalidate local identity, admitted custody, or gameplay.
- Model output is not a world event. AI, MCP, proof-brain indexes, receipts, and UI projections cannot sign, admit, or replace authority.
- Existing v120 HTTP routes, proof objects, idempotency coordinates, bearer receipt schema, and compatible stored state are not renamed by the application v8 version.

## Apply the update

Installed clients use `v8.0.0-r1` as the new service-worker release coordinate. When an update is available, **Apply update** preserves current state for a frame, asks the waiting worker to activate, and reloads once on controller change. A failed or redundant worker remains retryable; update failure does not change Proof Object authority or gameplay state.
