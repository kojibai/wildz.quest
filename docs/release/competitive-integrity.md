# Competitive integrity contract

This document defines what a Wildz competitive result must prove. It is doctrine, not a claim that production admission, tournament telemetry, or remote publication has already run.

## Authority chain

1. Kai Klok is the primary temporal root. Canonical coordinates use exact safe-integer `uPulse`; ISO is descriptive display metadata and never ordering authority. Causal descendants advance parents, deliberate same-`uPulse` appends use sequence, verified divergent siblings may compare greatest admitted `uPulse`, and an equal-slot conflict fails closed.
2. A fighter is reconstructed from an SDK-verified exact card and revision, not caller-shaped stats. The immutable base proof remains unchanged while append-only creature history binds the exact asset, causal parent, event digest, resulting projection digest, idempotency, bounded effects, and admitted Kai coordinate.
3. An Arena replay binds the exact ruleset, definition, stage, roster revisions, Kai root, complete atomic input frames, checkpoints, terminal state, and transcript digest. Replay audit reproduces the terminal digest before considering settlement.
4. Ranked requires a verified signed global admission envelope covering that committed definition. Ranked consequences exclude permanent death.
5. Mortal requires the Ranked-grade evidence plus a verified signed covenant bound to the player, exact match, exact card revisions, mortality policy, nonce, and bounded Kai expiry. Consent is rechecked at settlement.
6. Admission, publication, rating, consequence append, and effect delivery are separate states. A local replay, receipt, queued proposal, or publication attempt proves none of the later states.

High-frequency inputs stay in the transcript. Only verified, admitted durable consequences append to the exact creature history and travel in standalone cards and Vaults. A Vault import must rebuild the same level, XP, bond, mastery, growth, condition, record, form, and life projection without rewriting the base proof.

A signed carrier proves carrier integrity, not a retirement. Imported retired cards require the exact origin receipt authority. Without it, Wildz retains the original artifact bytes and memorial-only card projection in retirement quarantine; the card is not playable, merge-authoritative, or marketable.

## Fair competition

- Fixed rules, canonical frame batching, deterministic seeded systems, bounded stage definitions, and pinned card revisions make replay meaningful.
- Ranked uses equal authored visibility and disclosed control/ruleset policy. Accessibility changes presentation, not hidden combat authority.
- Practice is non-settling. Adventure progression cannot silently become Ranked rating. Mortal retirement cannot leak into Ranked.
- Matchmaking, ratings, seasons, and tournament standings consume admitted results only. Local clocks, ISO timestamps, caches, AI classifications, and database rows cannot break ties or advance heads.
- Repeated input, stale revisions, expired covenant, mismatched roster, incomplete frame batches, replay divergence, or invalid signature fails closed.

## Tournament health and season review

A tournament-health audit reports ruleset/version distribution, verified/rejected replays, disconnects and forfeits, matchup distribution, rating movement, settlement latency, publication backlog, covenant rejection, unresolved divergence, and effect-delivery status. Every report names its observation window, sample size, missing data, and whether evidence is local, staged, admitted, or published. Season closure requires the current season head and an explicit confirmed command; an audit report cannot close a season.

Balance work may compare matchup outcomes, action selection, damage, stamina/focus economy, stage effects, comeback rates, match duration, and pick diversity. Small or biased samples stay labeled. No balance claim is production-proven until representative admitted telemetry and human playtests support it.

## Coaching, simulation, MCP, and AI boundaries

Coaching may offer opt-in recommendations from public or player-authorized replays. Deterministic simulation may test counterfactual balance changes. Coaching and simulation are not gameplay, matchmaking, rating, settlement, or proof authority; they cannot expose hidden opponent input or choose live-player actions.

MCP is a read-only audit and capability-inspection surface by default. It may help review artifacts, replay evidence, tournament health, publication state, and release readiness using installed v118 capabilities. Product-specific tools that do not exist must not be invented. AI and agents never sign or admit an Arena envelope, covenant, match, creature event, tournament result, publication, or release.

Tournament administration, publication, settlement, deployment, and release are command-only. They require a verified actor, scoped capability, current head/revision, causal parents, idempotency, and explicit confirmation of the exact reviewed command or permit digest immediately before execution. Plan, attempt, admission, and delivery are recorded separately.

## Release evidence required

The competitive release remains unqualified until automated tests and saved browser evidence prove deterministic replay, tamper rejection, Practice/Ranked/Mortal separation, exact-card and Vault history round trips, sunrise and night visibility, accessibility, performance budgets, and clean console/request behavior. Authenticated MCP reads, remote publication, tournament telemetry, and strict-live checks remain pending external evidence whenever credentials or production rails are unavailable. Documentation and passing local tests cannot be substituted for those results.
