# Wildz v11.0.0 qualification record

**Status: in progress. This branch is not a v11.0.0 release.** The package and installed app remain at 10.1.0 until the gates below are met together.

## Verified local work

- Exact signed-decimal world addresses, region-local movement and terrain, sparse atlas persistence, and bounded region generation have automated coverage, including distant addresses.
- New starters are Trail. Verified v10 cards retain their original rarity, proof, and proven version-3 first-meeting location when resaved with v11 continuity.
- The versioned rarity table gives each eligible site a chance in every band. Frontier Rare is exactly 1/1,000, Mythic 1/100,000, and Eternal 1/1,000,000. Signed encounter verification and deterministic creature birth replay work offline after an admitted encounter.
- The World, Creatures, and Conformance pages disclose the address model, creator grammar, odds, trust boundary, and synthetic conformance examples. Public example signatures use a key that cannot authorize player encounters.
- Capture presentation holds the in-world ball through draw-in and seal before opening the reward. Frame deltas are capped to prevent a stalled frame from skipping the motion; capsule rotation continues across the seal boundary.
- A verified v10 version-3 companion can recognize its first meeting place after leaving and returning. A choice appends one portable history event and one player recollection. The departure flag and completed event survive save/restore.
- The 2,844-test regression suite passed on 2026-09-26 (2,843 passed, one skipped); focused capture, homecoming, and exact-address site-scan tests passed. The repository's current `release:check` and optimized production build passed with the site-scan change, with existing dependency and image warnings. The local Receiz doctor reports `liveApi: needs-env`, so live admission remains unproven.
- Mobile browser review found and corrected a homecoming panel that overlapped movement controls. A browser check confirmed the panel stays hidden at the initial spawn point.
- Region-local scans now use a bounded exact-address search of generated v11 sites. A distant Rift destination cannot produce a v10 catalog hotspot or fabricate a v10 card; its site outcome remains unknown pending signed encounter admission. The scan includes neighboring regions only when the player is close enough to the edge to receive a clue.
- Public World, Creatures, and Rarity & Proof copy now presents one durable game experience without exposing internal release numbers or migration terminology. Exact rules remain available in expandable detail, and unnumbered example URLs coexist with the original test-vector paths. Compatibility and protocol versions remain internal engineering concerns.
- A local browser review at a 419-pixel viewport confirmed all three pages render without page-width overflow, user-visible version/migration text, or console warnings. The rarity table supplies a swipe cue and keeps class names visible while scrolling horizontally.
- Companion growth and card-back labels now describe the creature's history and original form in player language; internal migration terms are kept out of those game surfaces too.
- A browser encounter client boundary now submits an exact travel head before an exact site request, checks the returned actor and coordinates, and verifies the signed rarity and one-of-one birth locally. Focused tests cover ordering, tampering, mismatched identity, and offline failure. It is not yet connected to ordinary capture.
- New site results now pass an actor-scoped 12-per-minute durable issuance reservation; repeat requests for an already admitted site return the original signed result. This new authority path does not modify existing Receiz offline proof or historical card logic. Focused historical proof, Vault admission, and continuity tests passed after the addition.
- The full regression suite after that additive reservation passed on 2026-09-27: 2,849 tests, 2,848 passed, one skipped, zero failures. This confirms the covered existing behavior; device latency and the live service path still need separate qualification.
- The Rarity & Proof page can verify either a signed encounter result or a complete creature birth file locally, showing individual nature and body traits only when the entire birth replays correctly. A downloadable complete creature proof and focused tamper test demonstrate that path. Example files remain explicitly marked as examples.

## Release-blocking work

1. Wire admitted v11 site encounters into ordinary discovery and capture. Central legacy gameplay still creates v10 catalog cards; the new region-local scanner locates sites but does not yet admit or capture them. The published v11 odds are not yet the odds of ordinary in-game captures.
2. Carry the v11 birth proof and procedural anatomy through inventory, battle, renderer, card export/import, save, and ownership without weakening existing v10 verification.
3. Migrate remaining fixed-coordinate gameplay authorities and make old central terrain meet new regions without altering existing places. Region-local primitives and Rift support alone do not remove every reachable gameplay cap.
4. Finish offline pending observations, idempotent reconnect admission, trusted v10 travel origin bridging, and a live authenticated Receiz admission probe. The local environment has no live service token for that probe.
5. Complete embodied creature reactions and revisit/memorial variants for v11 births, transferred cards, and older cards with unknown proven meeting locations.
6. Verify a real capture playthrough and measure frame time, memory, active objects, traversal latency, and first playable time against the 10.1.0 baseline on the same devices. Automated phase tests do not prove zero hitches on all hardware.
7. Complete the release ledger from the first v10.0.0 release, update every app and service-worker version coordinate, and rerun release gates after all gameplay and proof paths are connected.

The v11 release commit, tag, push, and deployment must wait for these gates. The local incremental commits on `kojib/wildz-v11-release` are implementation checkpoints, not a public release claim.
