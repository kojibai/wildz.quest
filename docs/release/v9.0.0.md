# Wildz v9.0.0 — The Living Wilds

Application version: `9.0.0`. Installed PWA coordinate: `v9.0.0-r1`. Receiz integration: exact public `@receiz/sdk@126.0.0`, `@receiz/mcp-server@126.0.0`, and `@receiz/ai-skills@126.0.0`.

**Release preparation is in progress.** These notes collect the implemented post-v8 changes. Continuous construction and final release qualification remain in progress; this document does not attest publication or deployment.

## A world with physical geography

The post-v8 work adds deterministic streamed terrain, grounded movement, physically coherent landmarks and obstacles, underwater routes, aerial traversal, and safe landing checks. The living atlas preserves owner-scoped discovery while allowing free navigation. Aquatic and aerial encounters share canonical creature appearance with the rest of the world.

Creature capabilities now have individualized identities and structured progression, with unified controls for traversal, environmental support, and living work. Movement, companion changes, profiles, and the atlas also receive performance work that removes repeated persistence, upload, and verification from gameplay frames.

## An economy with persistent custody

The sovereign wallet projects from Receiz identity source objects and includes transfer recovery, recipient capability checks, exact card custody, and Wildz assets. Player messaging includes private multi-member rooms and transfer-linked conversations. Shared profiles and market settlement have additional integration work. Authenticated remote behavior requires its own observed deployment evidence.

The regenerative Grove introduces seasonal regional weather, bounded world emissions, creature work mandates, and persistent causal operations. Resource claims and shared stewardship build on exact material custody and cooperative world history.

## Continuous construction

The new construction work starts with renewable hay, timber, and stone, visible resource counts, and planning access with an empty satchel. Twenty-three component recipes specify exact material and work requirements for planned, framed, functional, and finished stages. Production placement rechecks source geometry, including aligned door and window openings.

Construction proof objects, durable local admission, the mounted builder, functional structures, renovation, personal blueprints, collaboration, and migration are being completed under the [approved construction plan](../superpowers/plans/2026-08-26-continuous-sovereign-construction.md). Final notes and verification will reflect the completed implementation.
