# Wildz v7.0.0 — Living Responsibility

Wildz `v7.0.0` is the public-product care-loop release on the exact `@receiz/sdk@120.0.0`, `@receiz/mcp-server@120.0.0`, and `@receiz/ai-skills@120.0.0` identity. It is deliberately additive to the `v6.1.0` baseline: the live proof head, conversation append, cross-platform memory, real neural performance, card/Vault custody, world movement, capture, roaming, and market settlement paths are not replaced.

Release date: 2026-08-17. Installed PWA coordinate: `v7.0.0-r1`.

## The recurring responsibility loop

An owner may explicitly awaken a creature's bounded roaming mandate. That same consent begins real care. Hunger and attention decline by deterministic whole-hour rules encoded by `wildz.creature-care.v7.0.0`; low needs reduce wellness, sickness follows, and zero wellness creates one irreversible condition transition to death in the exact card history. Calling a creature home pauses decline completely.

Players earn trail beans through the existing game. Feeding spends three beans, restorative treatment spends eight, and focused attention is free. Resource spending happens only after a valid exact-card append succeeds; rejection, insufficient resources, wrong owner, inactive mandate, malformed proof, or duplicate settlement writes nothing and spends nothing.

Care, neglect, relationships, discoveries, and roaming actions are append-only proof memory. The card face derives bounded saturation, foil, hue, and remembered-care expression from those admitted events. No timer mutates a base card, no model decides decay, and no server is authority.

## Device notifications

Care alerts require a direct user gesture and browser notification permission. The app computes a bounded future schedule from the exact local card proof in a background effect, sends only asset ID, bounded display name, threshold, time, and copy to a private service-worker cache, and registers the native `periodicsync` capability when available. The worker validates every field, deduplicates stable threshold IDs, and focuses the existing Wildz window when an alert is opened.

No proof object, identity key, conversation, Vault payload, access token, or authority is placed in the notification schedule. Notification availability is advisory: browser/OS background policy can defer delivery, and failed scheduling never delays or changes gameplay, care proof, conversation, capture, or voice.

## Market and ownership

The existing market remains Receiz-native end to end: explicit purchase confirmation admits a reservation, the buyer's connected Receiz wallet transfer is attempted first, the exact wallet ledger proof must match, and only the conditional settlement append changes ownership. A failed payment, missing capability, stale head, expired reservation, or unverified ledger event transfers no ownership. Settlement recovery reuses the exact transfer and idempotency coordinate.

Wildz does not treat a hosted/card checkout screen as ownership or silently invent a card-funding rail. A credit-card presentation may only be exposed when the configured Receiz payment capability returns its native checkout session; the proof-backed transfer and ownership append must still settle afterward. This keeps the proof object authoritative and prevents a UI success screen from becoming custody.

An uploaded and explicitly claimed bearer card belongs to the newest admitted owner. It leaves the former owner's active Vault projection, revokes the former mandate, preserves its complete brain and conversation history, and chats using current-Vault membership evidence without demanding the historical owner's Receiz ID.

## Hot-path boundary

Care projection is pure bounded arithmetic. Settlement runs on initial mount, five-minute checkpoints, focus, and visibility return. Notification scheduling is a separate effect. Card appearance is a small proof-derived projection recomputed only when that asset changes. None of these systems enter the movement frame, pointer path, capture/sealing path, identity verification path, text/audio reply stream, proof-memory append, or Three.js render loop.

## Apply update

The service-worker `WILDZ_APPLY_UPDATE` handler now passes `self.skipWaiting()` to `event.waitUntil()`. This keeps activation work alive after the click. Controller change preserves current app state and reloads once; a redundant worker or bounded timeout remains retryable.
