# Construction terrain precedence

New surface houses and compounds use the same authored mountain floor as grounded movement. The old mountain envelope remains a spatial index; it is not a filled collision box. A mountain rising from a lake therefore supports a building at its actual dry floor instead of being classified by the seabed.

Surface foundations now cut or fill natural terrain beneath their admitted floor footprint. A placement in water raises that contact plane to at least 20 cm above the rendered water surface. Slopes and shoreline crossings do not invalidate an otherwise supported design. Real trees, rocks, protected structures, other admitted buildings, material availability, crew qualification and build reach still constrain placement. Arbitrary suspended poses do not supply ground contact.

The land has no separate saved authority. `projectWildsConstructionTerrain` reconstructs oriented pads from exact admitted creation sources and the paid physical stages of manual foundations, floors, paths, gardens and workshops. Equipped or unequipped portable tool and weapon assemblies never supply an earth pad. Destroyed or unavailable source geometry supplies no active pad. Nearby legacy buildings with real ground contact retain their footing plane when a new grading blend reaches them; unrelated legacy sources, including historically admitted airborne geometry, replay with their original heads and poses.

The preview, canonical admission and ground render share the same cut/fill law. The flat visual apron is 1.1 m beyond the floor and blends into the original landscape over the next 3 m. Runtime footing support uses the exact oriented plane through the floor and its 0.6 m capsule clearance, avoiding a higher mountain triangle at a doorway. Graded mountain fields, added local ground fields, streamed earth meshes and masked water surfaces reconstruct from those same pads after reload. Real terrain outside that bounded influence remains unchanged.

Only affected terrain tiles increase to 32 segments (37.5 cm spacing). The existing bounded terrain tile caches remain in use; replacement Three.js buffers are disposed, and surface textures are reused. The pad selector compares compact current source/component/contribution heads and the custody/consumption of embedded lots. Portable equipment and sources in other spaces are excluded before their heads or material references enter that signature. Clock changes, gear actions and unrelated inventory snapshots do not serialize old proof histories or rebuild unchanged terrain. Full source verification runs when those relevant signatures change.

Creation proof compilation and historical component/build rule heads remain unchanged for existing definitions. Preview pose grounding happens before quotation and sealing; the controller repeats the same normalization at its final persistence fence. The existing Receiz SDK inventory → matching MCP schemas → installed AI workflow inspection was completed before implementation. No Receiz native proof, database or schema substitution was introduced.

Verification covers the mountain-envelope regression, a dry mountain in a lake, a compound spanning a steep shoreline, water-center landfill, rejected floating poses, exact source replay, legacy airborne replay, custody withdrawal, equipment preview/commit/restore, protected objects and actual gameplay capsule passage through paid house doors. Focused checks compile separately from the shared build and write their generated output outside the repository.

The paid 28 × 24 m compound check restores the complete saved source and player save, composes Campaign's terrain and visual collision objects, and passes its 0.38 m player capsule through the 1.2 m doorway using both straight world-south movement and a southeast diagonal. Both paths settle on the 3 cm floor above the graded earth without mountain or water traversal rejection.

Player save restoration also reconstructs verified construction ground where a local pad actually affects the saved position. It preserves the admitted floor above that earth and the flooded state of a submerged outer blend. Without this step, natural mountain normalization can restore a player beneath a filled apron and reject the first movement as a steep climb. Distant pads, legacy terrain and interior space restoration retain their previous behavior.

Manual foundation/workbench placement should use the same raised dry contact in water. A tree or owned structure inside the footprint should still block the design; a portable bow or tool should not change the ground.
