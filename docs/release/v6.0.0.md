# Wildz v6.0.0 — Living Creature Continuity

Wildz `v6.0.0` turns every verified creature card into a persistent, proof-native living subject. A creature no longer begins as a blank chatbot at capture. Its species, discovery coordinates, genome, traits, base statistics, visual form, provenance, and pre-capture self-model form its innate identity; capture begins the append-only relationship with its owner. Every admitted event after that point can become cited memory, so the creature speaks from what it has actually lived rather than reciting metadata or inventing a past.

This is a major release because the card, Vault, game runtime, Receiz Twin, memory model, voice, and animation pipeline now form one continuity system. The same exact creature survives export, import, ownership transfer, browser restart, autonomous time away, conversation, and future Receiz SDK/MCP surfaces without becoming a different NPC.

Release date: 2026-08-17. Receiz release identity: `120.0.0`.

## The accomplishment

The design has one non-negotiable direction of authority:

1. Verified proof objects establish the creature, its provenance, and its exact carried history.
2. The v120 proof brain indexes those exact objects and derives a subject projection; the index is not authority.
3. The Receiz Twin observes the subject and may decide how to speak or perform.
4. Model output is never a world event. Any consequential action requires a separate, valid command admission and state transition.
5. An admitted transition appends a new proof object. Only then may it become factual memory.

That distinction is what makes the creature feel alive without turning generated prose into game truth. It can remember a meeting because the meeting event exists. It can mention a keepsake because inventory changed through an admitted transition. It can be uncertain when evidence is absent. It cannot manufacture a trade, relationship, injury, discovery, transfer, or victory through dialogue.

## One creature, one brain

The observer route constructs a v120 subject from the exact verified card brain. The primary proof-object set includes innate identity, capture, every carried history event, prior conversations, and continuity events. The subject projection maps them into identity, canonical state, self-model, memory, relationships, inventory, abilities, condition, world position, mandates, event history, projections, and Wildz application namespaces.

The subject Twin receives proof references rather than a free-floating biography. Every turn also receives the exact current Kai `uPulse` and its causal geometry: authority class, temporal coordinate, lattice, beat, step, weekday, chakra, week, month, Ark, gate, elements, geometric teachings, semantic moment expression, creature location, owner world position, current condition, relationship register, latest lived experience, and causal head. This is conversation context, not mutation authority. It tells the intelligence both “who am I?” and “where in our living world am I now?” without allowing the reply to advance time or fabricate an event.

The response returns the subject head, proof-brain head, object Merkle root, object count, registry identity, reducer identity, Kai context, and the Twin decision. The Twin reasons freshly against the owner's actual words, current moment, retrieved memories, and state. It is explicitly instructed not to choose a stock response, offer a topic menu, repeat canned reassurance, or expose machine coordinates as dialogue. The deterministic phrase-template responder was removed. If real Twin intelligence is unavailable, Wildz appends no conversation and shows an honest retry state rather than presenting a template as intelligence. Proposed intents remain non-authoritative until a valid world command admits them.

### Innate memory and capture

Capture is not the creature's birth. Before capture, the creature already has an evidence-grounded self: what kind of being it is, the environment and conditions in which it was discovered, its genome-shaped tendencies, physical abilities, rarity, energy, temperament, and form. Capture is the first owner-relative episode. From then on, the exact owner relationship, conversations, exploration, encounters, victories, wounds, training, growth, discoveries, keepsakes, trades, evolution, and transfer can append to continuity.

The dialogue layer translates numerical evidence into embodied meaning. A creature does not announce a hash, schema, or raw score. It uses those values to choose confidence, rhythm, imagery, emotional intensity, vocabulary, and physical expression. Proof remains inspectable by the system while the player meets a being, not a database report.

### Memory truth

Conversation is itself continuity. A completed observer turn is appended to the card's exact history and becomes available to later turns. Memory retrieval is bounded and relevance-ranked, but factual statements require event citations inside the subject brain. Summaries and projections improve recall; neither can replace the cited source object.

Existing cards do not need to be redownloaded merely because Wildz upgraded to v120. Historical sealed bytes and stable v119 compatibility namespaces remain verifiable. V120 builds the subject projection from the exact carried evidence and preserves unknown namespaces byte-for-byte. A new download is needed only when the owner wants newly appended conversations or events carried in the portable artifact.

## Life while the owner is away

Creature autonomy is opt-in and bounded by an owner mandate. It permits non-financial exploration, meetings, relationships, discoveries, and keepsake barter. Scheduling and outcomes are deterministic from the subject, mandate, causal head, Kai coordinate, and bounded slot. On return, Wildz derives and verifies every due event, prepares all affected subjects, and commits the result atomically or writes nothing.

This release does not pretend a closed browser ran a hidden authoritative simulation. The current local runtime settles deterministic due events when the owner returns. V120 runtime jobs and world transaction surfaces provide the production coordination seam, but a queued job or generated decision is not a global commitment.

## Talk to the creature on its card

The Vault exposes direct conversation from the live card face. The experience combines:

- proof-grounded Twin response;
- a deterministic creature-specific voice identity;
- prewarmed local neural speech synthesis;
- browser-native speech only as a last-resort continuity fallback;
- waveform-driven mouth movement;
- genome-, temperament-, and state-shaped eyes, ears, tail, aura, posture, and motion; and
- conversation persistence back into the card brain.

Voice selection is deterministic per creature, so reopening or transferring the same subject does not randomly replace its voice. Delivery varies through the creature's state and expression without losing identity. The production path uses self-hosted, version-matched ONNX Runtime Web assets and Kokoro neural speech in Safari/WebKit. WebKit routes directly to WASM instead of waiting on an exposed but stalled WebGPU implementation. Model and voice assets prewarm outside the gameplay-critical loop; rendering and gameplay do not wait for synthesis initialization.

If local neural synthesis is genuinely unavailable, the browser voice can preserve audible response. The UI does not claim neural output when fallback ran, and neither speech engine receives authority over memory or state.

The intelligence rail is owner-scoped through Receiz ID. Wildz starts the official Authorization Code + PKCE flow and requests the v120 subject, subject-Twin, subject-memory, Twin, and World scopes used by this experience. Receiz returns the user-approved session to an HTTP-only cookie; the creature route never reads a player credential from an environment variable. The exact local v120 subject Twin reasons over the complete card proof brain. When a remote subject head is not yet published, the connected owner's Receiz ID Twin may supply live model inference over that exact proof context, but the local subject Twin still performs the proof retrieval, head binding, non-authority separation, and final result construction. Static dialogue is never accepted as a successful turn.

## Card and Vault portability repaired

The complete import path now treats binary artifacts as binary artifacts. Upload accepts the bounded raw or multipart transport the client actually sends, avoiding the prior `Failed to parse body as FormData` failure. Card-only artifacts and complete Vaults use the same verified inspection boundary, then take different merge paths:

- same-owner artifacts merge every verified card into the active Vault;
- a bearer artifact requires explicit claim confirmation;
- existing cards are preserved;
- exact duplicates are removed;
- compatible descendants advance causal history;
- structural conflicts fail closed; and
- the UI reports real success or actionable failure instead of silently doing nothing.

Large collection regressions cover full-card unions, including the historical 97/98-card boundary. Save uses the native Apple share/save surface when available and a raster/file fallback elsewhere, without placing a blocking download overlay over the game.

## Ownership and bearer transfer

V120's bearer-instrument surface is additive to the verified full-artifact claim baseline. Transfer preview, instrument issue, inspection, claim, cancellation, and status are capability-gated operations. A direct claim still independently opens the returned artifact and accepts only the exact admitted bytes. The ownership witness carries the current owner, previous owner, head reference, history digest, append count, witnessed Kai coordinate, and claim identity.

A transfer preserves the creature's subject identity and complete brain but revokes former-owner authority immediately. The previous owner's active Vault receives a non-authoritative invalidation projection and reconciles the transferred asset out; that projection does not create the transfer. The new owner's local card appears only after verified custody is admitted. Marketplace settlement remains fail-closed wherever a separate conditional market append capability is absent—bearer instruments are not silently treated as listings, payment, or settlement receipts.

## Reliability and performance

The work since v5 includes runtime checkpoints and save scheduling, exact Vault restore and merge repairs, ownership reconciliation, binary artifact transport, raster export fallback, local Twin continuity, observer fallback, neural voice, and expressive card animation. The hot gameplay loop gained no model inference, network wait, or proof-brain rebuild. Subject construction and voice work occur on interaction or background prewarm boundaries.

The PWA coordinate advances to `v6.0.0-r1`, giving installed clients one release-distinct shell while preserving owner state across activation.

## Search and social discovery

V6 ships a production search surface without adding work to the gameplay render loop. Static root metadata provides one canonical URL, descriptive title and snippet, large-image preview policy, Open Graph and Twitter/iMessage cards, and a server-rendered accessible game summary. `VideoGame`, `SoftwareApplication`, `WebSite`, `WebPage`, `Offer`, publisher, and primary-image JSON-LD describe only claims visible in the product. Standard and image sitemaps expose the canonical game and cinematic discovery artwork; robots exclude private APIs and browser test fixtures while leaving required scripts and assets crawlable.

The social master is a bespoke 1200×630 composition built from the established emerald, gold, creature, explorer, card, and wilderness art direction. Its generated scene contains no generated typography: the official vector wordmark and exact campaign copy are composited deterministically. A separate text-free 1280×720 image is used for Google discovery and structured data, keeping the subject clear under landscape crops while avoiding promotional text in the indexed primary image. These assets are static and fetched only by clients or crawlers that request them.

This implementation creates strong technical eligibility; it cannot promise a particular Google position. Ranking and indexing remain search-engine decisions influenced by relevance, authority, competition, crawl history, and external references. Search Console verification, sitemap submission, URL inspection, and monitoring are post-deployment operator actions and require the site's verified Google property.

## Compatibility and migration

- Existing v5/v119 cards and Vaults remain inputs; they are not rewritten in place.
- Stable historical channel names, artifact headers, and idempotency namespaces remain stable when changing them would break retries or cross-tab reconciliation.
- New v6 bearer receipts use `receiz.wilds_bearer_claim.v120`.
- New living-subject observations expose the v120 subject and proof-brain coordinates.
- Historical runtime admissions, capabilities, plans, confirmations, receipts, or Connect tokens cannot authorize a v120 operation.
- Unknown carried namespaces are preserved byte-exact.

## Verification standard

Source qualification requires the complete `pnpm release:check` gate: all Node tests, typecheck, v120 repository checker, MCP conformance, warning-free lint, secret scan, optimized production build, and Receiz doctor. Focused browser qualification additionally exercises card/Vault upload, direct creature conversation, neural audio production, mouth events, and the PWA shell in production mode.

Passing local verification establishes source and integration evidence. It does not fabricate production credentials, strict-live remote admission, external deployment, representative-device certification, payment settlement, or a globally published release. Those remain separately observable gates and fail closed when unavailable.

Wildz adds no external database. Owner continuity remains in the verified local Vault/IndexedDB boundary; configured Receiz rails carry shared authority. Offline access is intentionally narrow: the installed shell and previously visited public profiles and previously visited public cards may remain readable. Sign-in, live world activity, creature intelligence, market operations, publication, transfer, payment, and settlement require a connection and the exact required capability.

## Why v6 matters

Wildz now demonstrates a general pattern beyond games: a portable subject can have an identity that outlives an application session, a memory that is grounded in admitted events, an AI observer that cannot rewrite truth, and relationships that remain continuous across custody changes. The emotional experience and the integrity model reinforce each other. The creature becomes more personal precisely because its history is harder to fake.
