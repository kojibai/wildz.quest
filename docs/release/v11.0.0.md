# Wildz v11.0.0 — release draft

**Not yet qualified for publication.** Application and installed PWA remain at
10.1.0 while the [qualification record](v11.0.0-qualification.md) is open.
The [complete committed source ledger](v11.0.0-source-ledger.md) starts at the
first public v10.0.0 tag. This document must be updated from the final release
commit before the maintainer pushes or tags it.

## World and discovery

The world now has exact region addresses whose size is limited by the stored
address format rather than a fixed gameplay rectangle. Local movement, terrain,
the sparse exploration atlas and generated site search use those addresses.
Existing places retain their historical coordinates and world data. The World
page explains the geography and generation rules in player language.

## Creatures and rarity

Every newly issued starter is Trail. The signed encounter law gives every
eligible site a chance at each rarity class. At the frontier the exact chances
are Rare 1 in 1,000, Mythic 1 in 100,000 and Eternal 1 in 1,000,000. The site’s
distance from the start chooses the disclosed odds band; an individual signed
result fixes its creature and cannot be rerolled by a failed capture or retry.
New creature births derive their lineage, anatomy, color, temperament, voice,
abilities and starting attributes from their signed encounter. A card retains
that one birth identity across later history.

The Creatures and Rarity & Proof pages explain these rules without exposing
internal protocol versions. The proof checker can replay complete example
births offline and rejects altered examples. Example keys cannot issue real
player encounters. A local capture envelope proves the signed birth; it does
not independently attest live custody, ownership or a completed transfer.

## Living history and capture

Verified historical cards retain their original rarity, asset identity, proof
and history when resaved. A proven first-meeting location can produce a
homecoming when its companion leaves and returns. Capture presentation holds
the ball through draw-in and sealing before opening the reward; visual frame
deltas are bounded to avoid skipping the motion after a stalled frame.

## Performance and proof boundaries

Earlier 10.1.0 changes reduced first-frame and gameplay work. This release
continues to use bounded region generation and sparse state. Historical Receiz
offline verification remains intact; new birth verification uses a separate
async boundary. The release must record a same-device comparison of first
playable time, movement and capture frame pacing, memory and active objects
before any zero-regression claim is made.

## Required qualification before publication

Normal exploration must produce signed encounters and captured procedural
cards through combat, inventory, world rendering, export/import, saves and
ownership. Offline reconnect must admit pending sites without allowing
unearned frontier travel. The final source gate, real browser capture,
authenticated Receiz probe and same-device performance comparison must pass.
Then update the application, PWA and release metadata together, append the
final commit to the ledger and rerun the gates. The maintainer will push the
qualified local commit.
