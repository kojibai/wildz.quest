# Wildz competitive-alpha gameplay scorecard

This is an evidence-honest pre-release assessment against a world-class absolute bar, where `10/10` means the best shipped games sustain that quality with representative players, production telemetry, accessibility review, and performance evidence. It does not grade against legacy browser-game expectations. Scores are provisional implementation-confidence grades, not a best-in-market claim or a claim that unfinished release gates passed.

## Current grade

| User-facing scale | Weight | Provisional grade | Evidence and present gap |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Competitive mastery and agency | 16% | 7.8/10 | Deterministic movement/combat, stamina/focus, defense, tags, stage interactions, and tiered opponents create learnable decisions. Representative expert playtests, matchup depth, and input-latency evidence are not yet verified. |
| Competitive integrity | 14% | 8.4/10 | Mode separation, atomic replay, revision pins, signed-admission interfaces, Mortal covenant, and fail-closed settlement are strong architectural controls. Production issuer/admission and adversarial browser evidence remain pending. |
| Feel, clarity, and responsiveness | 12% | 6.8/10 | Existing controls and feedback are implemented, but frame-time, hit-readability, animation timing, and device-latency qualification are pending evidence. |
| Deterministic replay and spectator value | 10% | 8.2/10 | Committed definitions, canonical frame transcripts, checkpoints, and terminal digests support deterministic replay audit. Spectator presentation and long-session corpus evidence are not yet proven. |
| Portable creature continuity | 10% | 9.0/10 | Exact append-only history can preserve level, XP, bond, mastery, growth, condition, record, form, and life state across card/Vault transport without rewriting the base proof. Full production artifact round-trip evidence is still required. |
| World realism and atmosphere | 9% | 7.7/10 | Kai-rooted sunrise at `00:00:00`, continuous day/night expression, deterministic stars, moon readability, and a heading-following lantern form a coherent world law. Chromium deep-night readability is visually verified; broader device/art-direction review remains. |
| Accessibility and control inclusion | 8% | 7.3/10 | Reduced-motion/quality paths, labels, touch sizing, automatic darkness lighting, and a 390 px overflow check are verified. Contrast, zoom, screen-reader, controller, and current mobile WebKit evidence remain pending. |
| Balance, progression, and long-term motivation | 8% | 6.2/10 | Exact-card bond/mastery/growth and separated competitive modes support meaningful progression. Economy pacing, meta diversity, retention, and anti-grind validation require longitudinal playtests and admitted telemetry. |
| Performance and reliability | 7% | 6.0/10 | Deterministic pure systems are testable and visual quality scales, but production frame-time, memory, thermal, network-recovery, and long-session budgets remain pending evidence. |
| Live operations and tournament health | 6% | 5.5/10 | Admission/audit doctrine and tournament-health measures are defined. Live seasons, representative telemetry, incident drills, publication latency, and operator evidence are not yet proven. |

The weighted provisional implementation score remains **7.5/10** after the limited browser evidence update. The honest world-class experience grade remains **not yet verified** until performance, accessibility, strict-live, multi-device, and representative-player gates complete. A code-complete feature cannot earn a best-in-market score from architecture alone.

## Gap-closure program

1. Prove the integrity spine: complete deterministic Practice, Ranked, and Mortal replay corpora; tamper every signature, revision, frame, Kai coordinate, history link, and terminal digest; retain the rejection evidence.
2. Tune competitive mastery with novice, intermediate, expert, and accessibility cohorts. Measure action legibility, counterplay, matchup spread, skill-expression ceiling, frustration, rematch intent, and controller/device parity.
3. Establish balance safely: run deterministic simulation for hypothesis generation, then validate against admitted human matches. Keep coaching opt-in and advisory; neither simulation nor AI becomes authority.
4. Qualify feel and performance on target desktop and iPhone-class WebKit devices, including deep night, lantern, Arena, resize, offline/reload, long sessions, thermals, memory, frame pacing, and input latency.
5. Verify portable creature continuity through standalone-card and full-Vault export/import on a fresh compatible user, including exact history, terminal states, duplicate idempotency, fork rejection, and byte-identical base proof.
6. Run accessibility review for contrast, non-color signaling, labels, focus, zoom, reduced motion, touch, keyboard, controller, and equal Ranked visibility.
7. Exercise tournament and live-operations procedures with sanitized evidence: admission health, replay rejection, disconnect/forfeit rates, rating distribution, publication backlog, settlement latency, covenant failure, incident response, and season-close confirmation.
8. Re-score only from retained evidence. Any missing production rail, credential-dependent strict-live check, MCP audit, remote mutation, or player-study result stays a named non-pass rather than an inferred success.
